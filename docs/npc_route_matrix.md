## Reading-first authoring rule

For each major event, add 2–4 interludes before a decisive branch: travel,
letters, work, meals, rumor, silence, or aftermath. Most interludes should have
one *Continue* action; only use multiple choices when the player is changing a
relationship, learning optional information, or giving something up.

The other Signature NPCs are registered and can be referenced now, but they do
not yet have six-to-twelve authored encounters. They are explicitly backlog,
not falsely marked complete.
